Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to SleekPOS. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

By using SleekPOS, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following personal information:

  • Email address — collected during account registration and sign-in.

We do not collect any other personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or physical address unless you voluntarily provide it within the app.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

The data we collect is used solely for app functionality, including:

  • Authenticating your identity and managing your account.
  • Enabling core features of the app such as shop management, sales tracking, and receipt generation.
  • Communicating important updates related to your account.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

SleekPOS uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — to manage user sign-in and account security.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore — to store and sync app data securely.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — to collect anonymous crash reports and improve app stability.
  • Firebase Analytics — to understand app usage patterns and improve the user experience.

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Modification — You can view and update your account information within the app at any time.
  • Deletion — You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided at the bottom of this page.

6. Cookies & Tracking

SleekPOS uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as session duration, screen views, and feature usage. This data helps us understand how the app is used and identify areas for improvement.

No advertising trackers or third-party cookies are used. The analytics data collected is anonymous and cannot be used to personally identify you.

7. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure, which complies with industry-standard security practices.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

When we make changes, users will be notified through the Google Play Store update notes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
